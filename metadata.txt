about=A guided side panel (in German) leads urban design students from an empty map to a clean data basis in 8 steps: choose a coordinate system and a study area, load data (OpenStreetMap, official ALKIS data from the open services of the German states, or own GeoPackages), check hints, choose columns and sources, and create ordered result layers, a simple accessibility check and a PDF map. Raw data is never changed; uncertain cases are shown, not fixed silently. Created in the elective course "Vibecoding KI Werkstatt" at Jade Hochschule (Urban Design, winter term 2026/27). Experimental: tested mainly in Oldenburg (Lower Saxony).
